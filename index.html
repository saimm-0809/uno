MYSTERY EGGS

Put cards in the nest and hatch.

CARDS

HOW TO PLAY

Choose a bet and hatch an egg. What hatches decides your prize: Pebble 0x (lose), Chick 1x (cards back), Blue bird 2x, Unicorn 3x, Dragon 10x. Default odds: 55%, 22%, 13%, 7%, 3%. Average payback is about 99%, so it is close to fair but the house is slightly ahead over time.

HIGHER OR LOWER

Bet and deal a card.

CARDS

HOW TO PLAY

A card from 0 to 9 is dealt. Guess if the next card will be higher or lower. The payout depends on the risk: a safe guess pays little, a risky guess pays a lot (shown on the buttons). A tie gives your bet back. The house keeps 4% on average. Your card stays dealt if you leave, so you can finish it later.

COLOR SPIN

Pick a color, set a bet and spin.

CARDS

HOW TO PLAY

The wheel has 25 spots: 6 red, 6 yellow, 6 green, 6 blue and 1 WILD. Pick a color. If the wheel stops on your color, or on WILD, you win 3.4 times your bet (rounded down). Chance to win: 28%. Payback is about 95%.

Set a bet and pull the lever.

CARDS

HOW TO PLAY

Three reels with R, Y, G, B, W (wild) and +4. Pays: three of the same color 12x, three W 30x, three +4 100x. Any two matching symbols give your bet back (1x). Symbols W and +4 are rarer. Chance to win something is about 50%. Payback is about 95%.

HOW TO PLAY

Open a duel and your stake is held. The other player can accept it by matching the stake. The server flips a fair coin and the winner takes both stakes. There is no house cut. You can cancel your own duel any time before it is accepted and get your stake back.

CARD PACKS

Collect every Uno creature. Pick a pack and open it any time. The Ultra Wild Pack is the hardest to crack.

MY COLLECTION

SHOW

HOW IT WORKS

There are loads of creatures in 6 rarities: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary and the super rare MYTHIC. Each card has its own value. Skip Pack is cheap and mostly commons. Reverse Pack guarantees an uncommon or better. Wild Draw Four Pack guarantees a rare or better and holds some cards you cannot find anywhere else. The Ultra Wild Pack costs a lot, guarantees an epic or better and is the only way to get a MYTHIC (about 1 in 80 cards). SPECIAL cards can only be found in one pack. Sell any card back for its value, or sell all extra copies at once and keep one of each. Your collection value counts toward your net worth. Click a card to see it up close, and move your mouse over a card to tilt it.
